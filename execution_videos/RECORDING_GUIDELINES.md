## 1. Quick Recording Tools (Windows)

You can record your execution video easily using built-in Windows tools or free software:

### Option A: Windows Built-in Screen Recorder (Easiest)
- **Shortcut:** Press `Win + Alt + R` to start recording immediately.
- Press `Win + Alt + R` again to stop recording.
- The recorded `.mp4` file is automatically saved to your `Videos/Captures` folder.
- Rename and move it to this folder: `execution_videos/execution_demo_video.mp4`.

### Option B: OBS Studio (Best Quality)
- Download and open [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/).
- Add a "Display Capture" or "Window Capture" source.
- Ensure your microphone is enabled and tested.
- Click **Start Recording**, follow `DEMO_WALKTHROUGH_SCRIPT.md`, and click **Stop Recording**.
